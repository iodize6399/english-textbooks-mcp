46.7

TYPES

Two other important occasions where *that* is preferred to *who* are:

After an interrogative.

Who that understands music could say his playing was good? (More usually found in writing than in speech.)

After *much*; *little*; *few*. (Compare *all*, *any*, *only*, Exercise 46.3.)

The few that came were enthusiastic.
