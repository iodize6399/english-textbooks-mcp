For difference between PRESENT SIMPLE and PRESENT CONTINUOUS see Exercises 21.7–9.

21.3

Elementary

PRESENT SIMPLE. This tense occurs in speech far less frequently than the Present Continuous. It doesn't really describe PRESENT action, but something permanent or habitual.

Question-word *when?* cannot be used with PRESENT CONTINUOUS tense, except when this tense is used to express immediate future.
