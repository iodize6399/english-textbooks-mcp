## 19 Short-form Responses using Auxiliary Verbs

### 19.1 Elementary

The most important function of the auxiliaries, or special finites, is their use in short answers and responses.

Are you fond of fish? No, I'm not. Yes, I am.
Do you like fish? No, I don't. Yes, I do.

Contracted forms are always used.
