41 Perfect Infinitive of Imaginary Past

(2) I should have liked to go.
(3) I should have liked to have gone.

The type (3) is very clumsy, but strangely enough quite commonly heard. The other two are logically preferable, and the first is the more frequently heard. (See also notes to Exercise 41.2.)

Must, meaning 'is (logically) so', makes its past by adding a perfect infinitive.

He must be a fool. (= he certainly is!)

He must have been a fool. (= he certainly was!)
