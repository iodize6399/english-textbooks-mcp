Fairly is never used with comparatives

I did it rather better last time.

Fairly is never used with colours, unless the colour is modified by light or dark.

(a) This one's a fairly light green.
(I think it's light enough for you.)
(b) This one's a rather light green.
(It won't do for you; you want something darker.)

(Phoneticians please note the rising tail of (a) and sudden fall on light in (b); similarly in all these fairly-rather sentences.)

(c) Your nose is rather blue.
(but you don't want it to be.)

Notice the alternative word-order with rather:

I've got a rather bad cold.

I've got rather a bad cold.
