### 19.17

#### Elementary

#### Introduction to QUESTION-TAGS.

This device is treated fully in Section 32, and the following exercises are merely to acquaint the student with the form, so that it can be used for certain responses.

To turn statements into rhetorical questions (cf. French *n'est-ce pas*, German *nicht wahr*, etc.), in English, the appropriate auxiliary is used as a tag at the end of the remark.

An AFFIRMATIVE remark has a NEGATIVE tag.

You are coming tomorrow, aren't you?

He has seen it, hasn't he?

You know her, don't you?

A NEGATIVE remark has a POSITIVE tag.

You don't know, do you?

They won't find it, will they?
