ADVERB PARTICLE: He couldn't get his talk across.
(= manage to communicate)

He couldn't get it across.

PREPOSITION: He couldn't get across the river.

He couldn't get across it.

STRESS. The difference is quite clear in speech. At the end of a phrase a VERB WITH PREPOSITION has a final stress on the VERB:

Give it to the man you 'spoke to.

Who does he 'take after?

At the end of a phrase a VERB WITH ADVERB PARTICLE has a final stress on the ADVERB:

Which word are you going to look 'up?

This is the book he brought 'back, and here's the one he wants to take 'out.

PHRASAL VERBS with prepositions also take their final stress on the adverb when in this position:

We have a lot of troubles to put 'up with.

Chess is a good game to go 'in for.
