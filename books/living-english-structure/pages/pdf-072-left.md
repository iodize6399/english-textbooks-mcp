28 The Future and Future Perfect

The FUTURE PERFECT CONTINUOUS tense is not very often used.

By Christmas I shall have been working in this office for ten years.

– and I shall presumably continue to work here

Intermediate

Look at this pair of sentences:

*He won't come till the play begins.*

This means that he will come just at the beginning of the play.

*He won't come till the play has begun.*

This means that he will arrive *after* the beginning.
