# 44.6
Elementary

PREPOSITIONS. When a preposition governs an interrogative, it is placed at the end of the sentence in spoken English.

I was speaking to him just now.

Who were you speaking to?

What and Which are treated in the same way.

A table is made of wood.

What's a table made of?

This is a part of the table.

What's this a part of? (= Possessive case of things.)

Note also the double use of what for? in spoken English.

What's this money for? (= for what purpose?)

What are you crying for? (= why?)
