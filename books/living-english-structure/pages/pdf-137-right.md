# REPORTED SPEECH (EXCLAMATIONS)

# 51.15
Intermediate

Reporting an exclamation is usually best achieved by a circumlocution reflecting the spirit of the original exclamation.

Exclamations are not often reported in spoken English, so too much time should not be wasted in hunting for the best expression. The other forms of REPORTED SPEECH are far more important.

Some exclamatory forms are really questions (rhetorical) or imperatives.

'What a lovely garden (this is)!'

He remarked what a lovely garden it was.

'Hello! where are you going?'

He greeted me and asked (me) where I was going.

'Oh dear! I've torn my frock.'

She exclaimed bitterly that she had torn her dress.

or: She sighed and said that she had torn her dress.
