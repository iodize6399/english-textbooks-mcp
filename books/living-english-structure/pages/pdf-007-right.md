# 1.4
Elementary

UNCOUNTABLE nouns, and COUNTABLE nouns in the plural, are preceded by some when 'a certain quantity, or number' is implied.

Bread is good for us. (All bread, in general.)
Give me some bread. (A certain quantity.)
