## 41 Perfect Infinitive of Imaginary Past

41.1
Intermediate

The form to have eaten takes the place of the infinitive when certain constructions, notably is to, ought to, like, are used for imagined action in the past.

He is to come tomorrow.

He was to have come yesterday . . . (but he didn't).

Note that I should like to go has the past forms:

(1) I should like to have gone.
