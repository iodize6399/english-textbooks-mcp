# 34.2
Intermediate

I hope and I am afraid take not after them as a negative response.

Think and believe may also use this device, especially when emphasis is required.

I wonder if they've got lost? -

(1) Oh, I hope not.
(2) Oh, I don't think so. (I think not.)
(3) I'm afraid so. (I'm afraid they have.)
