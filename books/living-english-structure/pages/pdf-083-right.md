## 32 Question-tags

### 32.1 Elementary

A very common device in conversation is that of making a statement and at once asking the listener to confirm it. In most languages this is done simply by means of a stereotyped phrase. (Cf. n'est-ce pas, nicht wahr, non è vero, ¿no es verdad?, nu-i aṣa, že ano (ze ne), nie prawda-li, не так-ли, **اليس كذلك** etc.)

In English this has to be practised, as we have a variable form.

Positive statement, negative tag; negative statement, positive tag.

The auxiliaries repeat themselves in the tag. Other verbs use *do* in the tag.

He is here, isn't he? (He isn't here, is he?)

You will come, won't you? (You won't tell him, will you?)

He came yesterday, didn't he? (He didn't go, did he?)
