# REPORTED SPEECH – STATEMENT

51.4

Elementary

When the reporting verb is in the PRESENT SIMPLE, PRESENT PERFECT, or FUTURE SIMPLE tense, there is no change of tense in the words reported. Notice only the necessary change of person.

'I am very sorry.'

He will tell you

He says

He has just told me

(that) he is very sorry.
