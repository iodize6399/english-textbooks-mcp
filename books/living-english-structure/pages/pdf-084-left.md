32.2
Intermediate

The positive forms *need* and *dare* are rarely heard in the affirmative with question-tags, but when so used, are treated as full verbs.

You need to come earlier, don't you? (= must)

*Have* and *have to*. See notes to Exercises 14.3 and 18.1.
*I am* usually has the tag *aren't I?* (for intonation see Exercise 32.3.)

*Used to* has a question-tag with *did*.
