# 1 Countables and Uncountables

## 1.1 Elementary

Nouns naming things that we cannot count (UNCOUNTABLE or MASS nouns) have no indefinite article, and usually no plural.

ink, water, wood.

* * *

1. A dog is an animal.
2. A potato is a vegetable.
3. A student is not always good.
4. A chair is made of wood.
5. A husband is a man.
6. A pencil is like a pen.
