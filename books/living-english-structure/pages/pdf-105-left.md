## 44 Interrogatives

44.1
Elementary

See also preliminary Exercise 8.1.

SUBJECTIVE CASE:
Who for persons in general – pronoun
What for things in general – pronoun
What for persons or things in general – adjective
Which for restricted persons or things – pronoun and adjective

Who broke my pencil? (of all possible people)

What has happened? (of all possible things)

What books are these?

What people live in this country?

Which of you can answer my question? (limited choice)

Which house is it, No. 32 or No. 34? (limited choice)

Of course there are occasions when the general and restricted interrogatives seem equally good, and the choice then depends on the speaker's point of view.
