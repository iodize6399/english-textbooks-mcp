CONTRARY

ADDITIONS

TO REMARKS

Here the appropriate auxiliary is introduced by *but*.

He can read French. – But I can't.

The stress is generally on the SUBJECT of the addition.

Notice that in all these additions to remarks, the added section may be spoken by the original speaker or by a second person.
