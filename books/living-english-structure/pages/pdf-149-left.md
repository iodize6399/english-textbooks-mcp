53 Miscellaneous Exercises

It wasn't at all a bad play.  
 They weren't half glad to be home again.  
 Similar mistakes are not uncommon.  
 (and other such double negative ideas)  
 QUESTION: Would you like another cup of tea?  
 RESPONSE: Not half!  
 Well, I wouldn't say no.  
 I shouldn't object.  
 Ra-ather! (wave-intonation)  
 I don't mind (if I do).

This understatement use of *rather* is a diffident way of expressing *very*, especially where the Englishman hates to be definite on a subject of a complimentary, eulogistic or emotional nature.
