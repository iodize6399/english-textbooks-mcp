47 Relative and Interrogative Links

An infinitive phrase is usual when the subjects of both sections are identical, the sense of the infinitive phrase being future. Compare:

I don't know which to take.

I don't know which I took.

Note also verbs of request:

Tell him where to go (= where he must go).

Ask him what to do (= what you must do).

Who is used in both subjective and objective cases.
