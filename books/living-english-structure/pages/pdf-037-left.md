## 20 Imperative

### 20.1 Elementary

The following is a simple drill, and should be carried out with BOOKS SHUT. The student should in all cases do as his colleague bids him. Teach the use of *please* before or after the imperative.

Imperative drills might well be divided and scattered over many periods; also combined with PRESENT CONTINUOUS tense drills. (See Exercises 21.1, 2.)

DRILL. BOOKS SHUT. Students to make the imperatives.
