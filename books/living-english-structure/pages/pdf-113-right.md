# 46.3

# **Intermediate  
DEFINING  
RELATIVE**

See notes to Exercises 46.1 and 46.2.

Although it was pointed out in Exercise 46.1 that for people custom prefers *who* in the subjective case, the following expressions almost invariably take *that*: **SUPERLATIVES**; *all*; *any*; *only*; *it is*.

She is the finest woman that ever lived.

He is the only American that has swum the Hellespont.

It's the teacher that decides what to read.
