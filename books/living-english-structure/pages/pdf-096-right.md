I couldn't deny that he'd made a reasonable excuse.
(I couldn't deny his having made . . .)

He denied knowing anything about the missing jewels.
It went on raining for days.

Do you recollect (my) telling John about the new house?

$^{1}$ And very many other transitive phrasal verbs, i.e. verb + adverbial (get off going to prison; talk over buying a house) and verb + preposition (see through his pretending to be an expert; get out of doing something unpleasant) combinations.

$^{2}$ The 'subjects' of the gerunds following the last three verbs can only be in the POSSESSIVE form; but as the effect is very heavy, it is more usual to employ a clause (see first example).
