## 36 Infinitive Implied by its Particle

### 36.1 Intermediate

A very odd but important idiom, particularly in spoken English, is the habit of finishing a phrase (usually a response) with the infinitive particle *to*, leaving the verb to be implied.

Will you show me how to do it properly? — I shall be glad to.
