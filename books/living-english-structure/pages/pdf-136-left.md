# REPORTED SPEECH (MIXED TYPES)

# 51.13
Intermediate

When statements and questions are mixed, each section must be introduced by an appropriate verb: tell, say, explain, remark, etc., for STATEMENTS ONLY; ask, enquire, want to know, wonder, etc., for QUESTIONS ONLY. A useful connective device for question plus statement is: adding that . . .

'I'm off to the pictures. Where are you going?'
He said he was off to the pictures and wanted to know where I was going.
