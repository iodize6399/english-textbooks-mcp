# CERTAINLY AND SURELY

# 53.6
Advanced

Certainly takes the same position as the frequency adverbs (Exercise 25.1); has the meaning 'I, etc., know for a fact,' 'it is definite'.

Surely can replace certainly, but is rarely found with this meaning. Coming at the beginning of a sentence, or less frequently at the end, or immediately after the subject if it is a pronoun, it has the meaning: 'I firmly believe, I very much hope, this to be true; it's not certain, but I feel confident it will probably happen.'
