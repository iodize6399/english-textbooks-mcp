**28.16**
**Intermediate**

Future (and past) of *can, must, needn't*. See also Sections 16 and 17.

*Can* has past *could* or *was able* (see Exercise 17.2), has future *shall/will be able*, or *can* (See Exercise 17.1).

*Must* has past *had to* (see Exercises 16.1-4), has future *shall/will have to*.

*Needn't* has past *didn't need to* or *needn't have* (see Exercise 16.3), has future *shan't/won't need to*, or *shan't/won't have to*.
