53 Miscellaneous Exercises

You're surely not going out in this rain!

Surely you're not going out in this rain. (= I should be very surprised if you did.)

I'm certainly not going out in this rain.

It's worth going to see, surely. (= I believe it is, don't you?)

It's certainly worth going to see. (= There is no doubt in my mind.)

He surely won't forget to bring it. (= I hope not.)

He'll certainly not forget to bring it. (= I know he won't.)
