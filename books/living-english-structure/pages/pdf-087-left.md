### 33.3 Advanced

The *got*-form of Exercises 33.1 and 33.2 is never found when the verb is in the IMPERATIVE, the FUTURE, or any of the PERFECT tenses.

Don't have any more!

I've had no news since Wednesday.

Nor in those phrases where *have* and its object represent a single semantic whole.

He had a very good journey.

Do you have breakfast at eight?

But we can say, *I haven't got time to do it.*

See also notes to Exercise 18.1 on the difference between *he doesn't have to* and *he hasn't got to*.
