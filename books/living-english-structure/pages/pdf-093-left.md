TOO, ENOUGH

38.3
Intermediate

See Section 10 for more elementary examples.

Too = excess; is a kind of negative.

It was too cold (for us) to go out.

= It was so cold (that)¹ we couldn't go out.

¹ That is usually left out in spoken English.
