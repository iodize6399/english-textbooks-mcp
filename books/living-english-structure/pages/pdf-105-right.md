# Elementary

OBLIQUE CASES. In the spoken language who serves for both SUBJECT and OBJECT, and whose for the POSSESSIVE. There is no possessive for what and which; for 'of what' and 'of which' see Exercise 44.6 on prepositions with interrogatives.

See Exercise 44.4 for further note on who? = whom?

Who saw you yesterday?

Who did you see yesterday?

— Note the inversion when anything but the subject is placed first.

Whose car is that? (Whose is that car?)
