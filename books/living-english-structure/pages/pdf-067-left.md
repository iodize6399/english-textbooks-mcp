Look at the two forms:

Will you come tomorrow? + please etc.
- a request

Will you be coming tomorrow?

- no request: a query about the future activities of the person addressed

* * *

● Make questions from the following statements in TWO ways: first making a request; then as a polite query on the statement.

EXAMPLE

You are going to see him tomorrow.

ANSWER 1: Will you (please) see him tomorrow?

ANSWER 2: Will you be seeing him tomorrow (by any chance)?
