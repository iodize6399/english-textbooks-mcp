Notice the following expressions:

- (a) It is time to go. (= The correct moment has arrived.)
- (b) It is a long time since . . . (= The time has been long.)
- (c) There is time to go. (= We have time; enough time exists.)
- (d) It's a long way to Tipperary. (= The distance between here and Tipperary is long.)
- (e) There's a long way to go yet before we get there. (= We still have this distance more to go; such a distance still exists for us.)
