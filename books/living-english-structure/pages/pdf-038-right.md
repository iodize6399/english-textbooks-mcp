## 21 The Present Tense

### THE REAL PRESENT – INTRODUCTORY DRILLS

21.1
Elementary

The PRESENT CONTINUOUS tense (REAL PRESENT), is a better introduction to the verb than the simple present. The verb *be* is already known, the *-ing* form of the verb is regular, and statements and questions in this tense have immediate practical value. It is obvious that the form *Do you write? Do you catch the chalk? Do you go to the door?* etc. cannot be easily practised, whereas the form *Are you writing, going to the door, speaking?* etc., can.

The elementary drill might well be combined with Exercise 20.1 on the imperative, by practising imperatives and asking about them in the present continuous tense.
