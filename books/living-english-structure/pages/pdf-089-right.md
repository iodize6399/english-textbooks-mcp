## 35 do and make

### 35.1 Intermediate

These two verbs often depart from their fundamental meanings of 'act' and 'construct' in idiomatic usage. Examples of fundamental meaning of do and make:

What are you making? – A cake.

What are you doing? – Writing a letter.

Idiomatic use of these two verbs can only be learnt by experience. The following two exercises practise a number of do and make idioms.
