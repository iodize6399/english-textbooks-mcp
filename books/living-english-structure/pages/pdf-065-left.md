28.7

Elementary

PRESENT

CONTINUOUS

FOR FUTURE

Like *going to*, the PRESENT CONTINUOUS can express a definite future, and its time is fairly immediate.

We're going to the pictures this afternoon.

She's buying a new one soon.

They're playing some Beethoven next.

This form is scarcely ever used with THINGS, or with verbs of Exercise 21.10.
