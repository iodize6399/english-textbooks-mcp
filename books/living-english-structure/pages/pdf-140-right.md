**52.2**
**Elementary**
**and**
**Intermediate**

When the verb in the active voice takes two objects, it is more usual in English to make the PERSONAL object the subject of the passive voice.

1 2

ACTIVE: Someone gave me a book.

PASSIVE: I was given a book.

¹ In the passive voice it is more usual to put an adverb of manner immediately in front of the past participle it qualifies: viz.— This chair is very well made.
