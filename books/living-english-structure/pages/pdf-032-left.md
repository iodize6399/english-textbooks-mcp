19 Short-form Responses using Auxiliary Verbs

19.7
Elementary
DISAGREEMENT

When we disagree with someone we respond with no or oh, no followed by the appropriate auxiliary. But may be used for disagreeing with a question or an assumption.

The box is open. – No, it isn't.

Why didn't you write to me? – But I did.
