Here is an old puzzle:

'The son of Pharaoh's daughter was the daughter of Pharaoh's son.'

It makes sense only if we read daughter-of-Pharaoh as one unit having the same meaning as Pharaoh's daughter.

Pharaoh (ruler of Ancient Egypt) is pronounced [ˈfeəraʊ].

## 8 Introduction to Interrogatives
who? what? and which?

### 8.1 Elementary

Interrogatives and relatives are dealt with as a group (Sections 44, 45), but a preliminary exercise or two, merely to practise the question-words, should come quite early. This exercise is restricted to the present tense and interrogatives in the subjective case. The uses of who? what? and which? should be clearly demonstrated on the blackboard.
