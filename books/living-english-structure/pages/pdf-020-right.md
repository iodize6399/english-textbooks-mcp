## 14 Negatives and Questions of Auxiliary Verbs

When these exercises are done orally, short forms should be used throughout.

AUXILIARY VERBS are sometimes called ANOMALOUS FINITES, SPECIAL FINITES, OR MODAL AUXILIARIES.

In the first year students should be acquainted with the following:

*be, have, do, did, can, could, must, shall, will.*

In the second year the others should be known:

*should, would, may, might, ought to, used to, daren't, needn't, had better, would rather.*
