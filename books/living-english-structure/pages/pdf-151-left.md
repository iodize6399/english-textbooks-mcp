## 55 The Comparison Game

### 55.1 Elementary

A simple free-construction exercise can be made by taking objects of a similar nature and asking the following two questions:

1 How is X like Y?
2 How does X differ from Y?

The student then makes a few sentences on those points of similarity or difference he can find.
