23 Present Perfect Tense

14 They are tired because they (work) in the garden since nine o'clock.

We sometimes use the PRESENT PERFECT CONTINUOUS merely to emphasize the fact that an action has been uninterrupted, even though it is not continuing now.

I'm cold because I've been swimming for an hour.
I'm very tired; I've been running round the town all day.
