53 Miscellaneous Exercises

parently correct grammatically is NEVER seen or heard in modern English; plenty of, a lot of, a great deal of, replace much and many in simple affirmative statements. Much is particularly objectionable in affirmative statements; many is uncommon.

Similarly far (= extent of distance) has a long way; far off (= position at a distance) has a long way off; and far away, far back have a long way away, a long way back in simple affirmative statements.
