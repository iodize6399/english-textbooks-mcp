AGREEMENT

See also Exercise 19.20.

The auxiliaries are commonly used when we agree with someone:

using yes for simple agreement.

It's very hot today. - Yes, it is.

using so for surprise.

Your glass is empty. - So it is.

using of course, etc., for something obvious.

They say we shall win. - Of course we shall.
