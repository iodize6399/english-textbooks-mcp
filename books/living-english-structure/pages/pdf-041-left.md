# PRESENT TENSE

# 21.7 Elementary

Make sure the fundamental distinction between the PRESENT SIMPLE and CONTINUOUS is well understood.
PRESENT SIMPLE. Habitual actions and general truths; not necessarily NOW.
PRESENT CONTINUOUS, the REAL PRESENT = NOW at this moment.
