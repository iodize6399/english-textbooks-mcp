FUTURE FORMS

He is going to do and he'll be doing have approximately the SAME MEANING, but the different grammatical form gives a DIFFERENT FEELING to each.

In the sentences:

(a) They are going to put on another play (soon).
(b) They'll be putting on another play soon.

(a) tells us the PRESENT intentions of the actors, (b) tells us of events at a future time, soon (which MUST be understood); it tells us of the RESULTS of the intention, but bypasses the intention itself.
