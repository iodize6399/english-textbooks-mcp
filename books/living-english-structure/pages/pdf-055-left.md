Elementary

See Exercise 26.3.

Remember that if the actions are consecutive they appear in PAST SIMPLE tense.

This is the same with habitual actions in the past.

I (always) wear a hat when it snows.

I (always) wore a hat when it snowed.

The PAST CONTINUOUS is used when we are not interested in the completion of one or more actions, but simply in the fact that they are in progress at the time something else happens.
