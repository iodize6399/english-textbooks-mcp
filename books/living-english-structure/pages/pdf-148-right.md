Rather as an understatement for very. Sentences like:

I've got some rather good news for you.

That's really rather clever of you.

I must confess I thought she was rather charming.

He showed me some rather fine landscapes by Constable.

obviously do not conform to the fundamental patterns of Exercises 53.7 and 53.8. This use of rather is part of the English love of understatement, a national characteristic that has affected our language idiom in many ways.
