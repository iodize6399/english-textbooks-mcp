19.18

Elementary
AGREEMENT
WITH
NEGATIVE

A form of question-tag, preceded by no, is used frequently here. Notice that the negative auxiliary and its tag both have a falling stress, never a rising one.

'He wasn't late last time.' 'No, he wasn't, was he.'

A question mark could perhaps be added, but the sense doesn't really demand one.

The teacher reads the statement; the student responds.
