It is a FUTURE PERFECT idea; 'by the time he comes, the play *will have begun*.' See Exercise 28.20.

In the same way as a FUTURE SIMPLE idea is expressed by the PRESENT SIMPLE in time clauses (Exercises 28.17–19), so a FUTURE PERFECT idea is expressed by the PRESENT PERFECT.
