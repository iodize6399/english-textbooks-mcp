## 38 Infinitive

### 38.1 Intermediate

Frequently a clause having the same subject as the main sentence can be more concisely expressed by using an infinitive.

I was glad when I heard of your success.

I was glad to hear of your success.

Notice also its use after a superlative.

He was the first man to climb Mount Everest.
