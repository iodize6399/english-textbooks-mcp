## 50 there is and it is

**50.1** *There is, there are.* This is the most usual way of
Elementary denoting existence in English, when the subject has not
PREPARATORY already been defined.

*THERE*

The two books are on the table. (We know which books.)

There are two books on the table. (Location of two unknown books.)

'Two books are on the table' is a grammatically possible but very rare form.
