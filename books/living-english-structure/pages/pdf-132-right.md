# REPORTED SPEECH (QUESTIONS)

# 51.7 Elementary

The word-order of REPORTED QUESTIONS is the same as a simple STATEMENT; there is no inversion as in a simple question. In questions introduced by a question-word (who, what, how, when, etc.), this word serves as a link between the introducing verb and the reported question. See also Exercise 47.3.

'What is your name?' ('My name is X')
He asked me what my name was.
