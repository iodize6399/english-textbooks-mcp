58.4

Here are seven short sentences or clauses:

a Just sit where you like.
b No, keep it as long as you wish.
c Just do as I tell you.
d Well, it's not so bad as I thought.
e The earlier, the better.
f In case it gets broken.
g Well, fasten them like I do. (See note in D above.)

● Use one of the above as a response to each of the remarks below. The teacher should make the remark, and the student choose one of the above seven phrases as a suitable response to it.
