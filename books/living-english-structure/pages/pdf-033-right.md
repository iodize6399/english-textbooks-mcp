# **19.12**
**Elementary**
**ADDITIONS**
**TO REMARKS**

Negative addition is made by using the appropriate auxiliary, introduced by *nor* or *neither*; INVERSION takes place.

He can't read this. – Nor *can I*.
Potatoes won't grow here. – Neither *will* roses.
