3.1 Explain *my – mine, your – yours*, etc., if necessary.
Elementary

* * *

● ALL BOOKS SHUT.

1 Is this my, your, her, our, etc. — paper, book, girl, etc.?
2 Is this book, paper, hat, parcel, girl, coat, etc. — mine, yours, his, theirs, etc.?
3 Whose is this?
4 Mix the three above types.
