## 29 The Past Perfect Tense

### 29.1 Elementary

The PAST PERFECT tense is related to a moment in the past in the same way that the PRESENT PERFECT is related to the present moment, i.e. it describes an action completed before some special past moment we have in mind.

# 29.2
Intermediate

The idea of the PAST PERFECT as a 'Before-Past' leads to its logical use in REPORTED SPEECH (see also later exercises on this topic). When the SIMPLE PAST I saw him, is reported in relation to the introduction he said that, it takes place notionally before the past said, and must therefore be PAST PERFECT.

Ago always dates back from NOW. Before dates back from any point in past or future, including NOW.
