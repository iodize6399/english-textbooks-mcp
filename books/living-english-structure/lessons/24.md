### 24.1 Elementary FORM OF PAST TENSE

The great majority of English verbs form the PAST SIMPLE TENSE and PAST PARTICIPLE by adding the sound [d], [t], or [id] to the stem – *serve, pass, want*. These are the regular verbs, sometimes called weak verbs.

The irregular verbs, as with most other European languages, include most of the verbs of the highest frequency. Those which change the vowel sound within the stem are often called strong verbs – *swim, break, lead*. Other irregular verbs add [d] or [t] and also change the vowel sound – *hear, sweep*. Verbs of one fairly large class already ending in [d] or [t] make no change – *spread, put*.
